    Linked Taiwan Artists Dataset is a valuable source of biographical resources and historical data regarding Taiwanese artists during the Japanese colonial period, spanning from 1895 to 1945. Through this dataset we get to know their lives, careers, and artistic contributions, as well as their participations in artistic associations, and involvement in exhibitions.

    As explained in the about us section of the dataset website, the data are collected from a diverse range of international databases including the Library of Congress, DBPedia, Virtual International Authority File (VIAF), and the Getty Vocabularies of the Getty Research Institute. Therefore, we developed a series of key questions to guide the analysis and extraction of the most relevant information from the dataset. These questions aim to provide an in-depth understanding of the Taiwanese artistic scenario during the Japanese colonial era, enabling the identification of trends, influences, and significant connections.
